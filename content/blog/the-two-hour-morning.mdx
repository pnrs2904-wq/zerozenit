## The uncontested window

Before the inbox opens, you have a window of attention no one else has claimed. Protecting it compounds faster than any app.

## Structuring the block

### One task, chosen the night before
Decide in advance so morning-you doesn't negotiate.

### No inputs until the output exists
Email and news can wait two hours.

## Surviving contact with reality

Some mornings collapse. The habit isn't perfection; it's returning to the block the next day.
