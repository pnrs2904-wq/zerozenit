## Why attention became the scarce resource

When machines handle the routine, the bottleneck shifts to the one thing they can't do for you: sustained, undistracted thought. The paradox of better tools is that they raise the value of the human work only you can do.

## Designing a deep-work block

### Pick the time before the day fills up
Reserve a fixed block — ideally first thing — and treat it as an appointment you can't move.

### Remove the exits
Close the tabs, silence the phone, and give the block a single concrete output.

## Measuring honestly

Track only two numbers: minutes of true focus and whether you shipped the one output. Everything else is vanity.

## A note on AI as a collaborator

Used well, AI clears the shallow work so your deep block starts sooner. Used poorly, it becomes one more thing to check. The difference is intention.
