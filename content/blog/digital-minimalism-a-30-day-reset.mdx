## The premise

Strip optional technologies for thirty days, then add back only what earns its place.

## The reset

### Remove
Uninstall the optional apps. Log out of what you can't uninstall.

### Rediscover
Fill the gap with offline sources of meaning you've been neglecting.

### Reintroduce
Add tools back one at a time, each with a rule for how and when you use it.

## After the thirty days

The goal isn't permanent abstinence — it's a deliberate relationship with tools that serve your values.
