## The dichotomy of control as a shipping strategy

Marcus Aurelius wrote to steady himself, not to publish. Founders can borrow the same move: separate what you control from what you don't, then act only on the former.

## What you control

### Effort and quality
The hours you put in and the standard you hold them to.

### Your response
Markets, competitors, and luck will do what they do. Your reaction is yours.

## What you don't

Timing, macro conditions, and how customers feel on a given Tuesday. Planning for them is wise; depending on them is fragile.

## Practicing the pause

Between stimulus and response there is a gap. Widening that gap is most of the discipline.
