## Flow is a state, not a mood

Flow is measurable: changes in attention networks, altered time perception, and a drop in self-referential thought. It has entry conditions you can engineer.

## The entry conditions

### Clear goals
You know what "done" looks like at every moment.

### Immediate feedback
The task tells you whether you're on track without waiting.

### Challenge slightly above skill
Too easy is boring; too hard is anxious. The sweet spot is just past comfortable.

## Engineering your day for entry

Batch similar work, remove decisions, and start with a small win to build momentum.

## What breaks flow

Notifications, ambiguity, and context switching. Guard against all three.
