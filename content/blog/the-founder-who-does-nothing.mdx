## Strategic idleness

Back-to-back calendars leave no room to think. The founders who compound advantages guard unscheduled time.

## Why empty time works

### Strategy needs slack
Good decisions rarely happen between two meetings.

### Reaction crowds out reflection
A full calendar means you only ever respond, never choose.

## Protecting the space

Block it, defend it, and resist the urge to fill it when it feels unproductive. That discomfort is the point.
