## Depth over count

Slow, deliberate reading of a few great books beats skimming a hundred. The count is not the point.

## How to read deeply

### Reread
The books that change you are the ones you return to.

### Annotate
Argue with the author in the margins. Reading becomes a conversation.

## Choosing what to reread

Pick the handful that repay attention, and let the rest go.
