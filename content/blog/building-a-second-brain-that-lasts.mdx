## Why note systems fail

They become collection hobbies. Capturing feels productive; revisiting is where value actually lives.

## A lighter approach

### Capture selectively
Not everything deserves a note. Most things don't.

### Connect as you go
Link ideas when you write them, not in some future cleanup that never comes.

### Review on a schedule
A system you don't revisit is a graveyard.

## The test

If you'd never reread it, don't save it.
