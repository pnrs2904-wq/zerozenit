## Good prompting is clear thinking made explicit

The people who get the most from AI can state a problem precisely, supply the right context, and describe what a good answer looks like.

## The three moves

### State the problem
Say exactly what you want, not vaguely what you're interested in.

### Supply context
Give the model what it needs and nothing it doesn't.

### Specify the shape of a good answer
Length, format, and constraints up front save rounds of back-and-forth.

## Why the skill transfers

These are thinking skills first and tool skills second. They outlast any single model.
