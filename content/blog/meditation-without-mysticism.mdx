## A plain account

You don't need incense or belief. You need a chair, a timer, and the willingness to notice when your mind wanders and return it.

## The whole technique

### Sit and set a timer
Ten minutes is plenty to start.

### Notice and return
When attention drifts, bring it back. That single move, repeated, is the training.

## What it does

Regular practice strengthens the noticing-and-returning circuit — the same one you use to resist distraction all day.
